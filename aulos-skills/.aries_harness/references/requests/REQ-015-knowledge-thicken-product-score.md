# REQ-015 — Raise craft further: knowledge thicken + product score dual-track

## Problem

SPEC-024 gave Catalog lock + family floors + chamber contracts. Cold guides can still
be **fact-thin** relative to the knowledge plane, and `eval_pass` still conflates
process honesty with **reader product quality**.

## Outcomes

1. **Knowledge thicken** — when Catalog `composer_id` is locked, pull knowledge-plane
   composer dossier (portrait / lifespan / summary / timeline → genesis) into Salon
   chambers; never overwrite richer work-specific craft.
2. **Work craft packs** — Catalog `work_id` may load a YAML craft pack (above family
   floor, below curated corpus) for cycle-specific listening craft.
3. **ProductScorecard** — separate reader-facing score (identity clarity, craft
   richness, bilingual parity, prose hygiene, atelier coverage). `eval_pass` follows
   product band; process scorecard remains diagnostic.

## Non-goals

- Building every composer dossier in this slice (ops/knowledge crawl).
- Replacing expert LLM review.
- Merging process + product into one opaque percentage.

## Acceptance

- Mapper tests: knowledge dossier → portrait/profile/genesis chambers.
- Product score fails when EN thesis empty / process locks present even if process pct high.
- Guide #50: product score recorded; knowledge or craft-pack thicken visible when assets exist.
