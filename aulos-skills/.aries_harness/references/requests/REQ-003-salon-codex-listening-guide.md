# REQ-003 — Salon Codex listening guide richness

## Problem

Current listening guides read as thin research notes (width bullets + depth bullets + map). They lack the density of an ideal classical **导赏**: portrait presence, genesis, oeuvre/history stature, anatomy, sound-world, famous interpretations, YouTube, and Discogs vinyl pathways.

## Desired outcome

Aulos guides should feel like a private salon program booklet: one listening thesis, museum-grade sections, curated media, and source hygiene — not a Wikipedia dump or AI card stack.

## Must include (when corpus-backed)

1. Work introduction + listening thesis
2. Work anatomy / listening map with ear cues
3. Composer profile + oil portrait (public-domain / credited)
4. Creation background, period, patronage/publication
5. Place in the composer’s oeuvre
6. Place in music history + why the work endures
7. Period instrument / ensemble context + modern listening modes
8. Kindred works
9. Famous interpretations (multi-era when relevant)
10. Appreciation videos (YouTube) + Discogs discography links
11. Practice listening path + myths/caveats

## Non-goals

- Inventing citations or fake Discogs/YouTube IDs
- Scraping paywalled liner notes
- Replacing curated excellence with ungrounded LLM prose
