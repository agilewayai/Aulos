# Dossier: BWV 988 — Goldberg Variations

dossier_id: bwv-988
work_title: J.S. Bach — Goldberg Variations, BWV 988
composer: Johann Sebastian Bach
era: Late Baroque (published 1741)
form: Aria with 30 variations + closing Aria da capo on a 32-bar bass

## Myths and caveats

- The Keyserlingk / Goldberg “insomnia night music” story is **reception lore**, historically debated — present as legend, not proven commission narrative.

## Width seeds

- Fourth part of Clavier-Übung; summit of keyboard variation craft
- Mix of courtly entertainment and encyclopedic technique (canons, genre paraphrases, hand-crossing)
- Reception: 19th-c neglect → early 20th-c revival → mid-century recordings as cultural monument
- Listening modes differ: harpsichord intimacy vs modern piano architecture vs HIP colors

## Depth seeds

- Lock ear on Aria bass before chasing surface figuration
- Variation grouping often felt in threes (character + canon); canons rise in interval
- Var. 16 French Overture = formal midpoint / second beginning
- Minor-mode cluster (15, 21, 25) as affective deepening; 25 as frequent nadir
- Quodlibet (30) as witty culmination; Aria return as memory
